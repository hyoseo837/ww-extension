How to Use
  1. Sign in with Google under Account below. New accounts receive 100 free credits — about ~200 job scans.
  2. Go to Profile & Context, drop in your application package PDF (from WaterlooWorks Documents), and click Extract Profile — the backend extracts your info and saves it to your account.
  3. Optionally add Custom Match Criteria (e.g. "Remote only, no Web3") to sharpen scoring.
  4. Navigate to the WaterlooWorks jobs list, click the purple AI Score tab on the right edge, then hit Scan All Jobs.
