v7.4.2 — Pricing revision

Goal

Revise the credit packages set in v7.4.0 (ADR 0034). The bonus-package model and the ~1 credit/scan margin are unchanged — only the tier amounts move:

idprice (cad_cents)creditsbonus
credits_300$3 (300)300—
credits_525$5 (500)525+25
credits_1100$10 (1000)1100+100

Why drop the $1 tier

Live testing surfaced it: Stripe's fee is 2.9% + $0.30 per charge, so a $1 payment nets only ~$0.67 — the fixed $0.30 eats a third. A $3 minimum makes the fixed fee a far smaller share (~$0.39 fee → ~87% kept, vs 67% on $1), while staying student-friendly. The bonuses were also trimmed (50→25, 200→100) now that the entry sits higher.
