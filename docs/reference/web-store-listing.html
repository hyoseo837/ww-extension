Description (current)

Rewritten 2026-06-12 for the 8.13.7 upload. Plain text — the store field is not HTML. 

WW Extension saves you hours of reading job descriptions by using AI to score WaterlooWorks co-op postings directly against your profile. WaterlooWorks dumps hundreds of postings on you each cycle; this extension reads each one, compares it with your resume and preferences using AI, and gives you a clear 1-20 score and verdict so you know exactly which jobs deserve your application - without opening 200 individual tabs.

Built for University of Waterloo co-op students: sign in with your UWaterloo (Microsoft) account to get started. New accounts include free credits to try it out, and you can top up anytime - scoring runs on our backend, so there's no API key to manage, and a confirmation always shows the estimated cost before a scan spends credits.

Features:
* Bulk scan: one click scores every posting in your current search - on both the Full-Cycle and Employer-Student Direct boards - and injects 1-20 scores directly into the job table.
* Ranked sidebar: all your scored jobs sorted best-first, each with the AI's reasoning and a point-by-point breakdown of what raised or lowered the score. Click any entry to jump straight to its row.
* Eligibility gates: postings that rule you out - a citizenship or work-authorization requirement you don't meet, or anything you declared off-limits - are marked Excluded so you don't waste time or credits on them.
* Your profile, your criteria: upload your application package once on the web dashboard, answer a short preferences wizard, and tell the scorer what matters most in your own words ("remote is non-negotiable", "never pure QA").
* Bulk save: filter by score threshold and verdict, then push the matching jobs into a WaterlooWorks folder in one shot.
* Feedback loop: rate any score with a thumbs up or down to help improve scoring quality.

Note: This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the University of Waterloo or WaterlooWorks.

Privacy practices form

  • Single purpose: Score WaterlooWorks co-op postings for the signed-in user against their profile.
  • Data the extension collects / transmits: account email + session tokens (authentication); job-posting text, titles, and employer names, sent to the backend only to produce a score; optional 👍/👎 score feedback with an optional comment (plus the posting text on a 👎), sent to improve scoring quality. The CV / application-package upload and match preferences are handled on the companion web app (ww-extension.hyoseo.dev), not by the extension; payments are Stripe Checkout on the web app — the extension never touches payment data.
  • Dashboard data-type checkboxes: tick Personally identifiable information (email), Authentication information (session tokens), and Website content (job-posting text from waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca). Everything else — health, financial and payment, personal communications, location, web history, user activity — is not collected by the extension.
  • Privacy policy URL: https://ww-extension.hyoseo.dev/privacy (in-app page; covers Microsoft sign-in, scan records, score feedback, Gemini processing, Stripe, Sentry, and the referral program as of v8.12.2 — verified in sync 2026-06-12).
  • Attestations: data is not sold; not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the single purpose; not used for creditworthiness / lending. (Matches the live policy.)
