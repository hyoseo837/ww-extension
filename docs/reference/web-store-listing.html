Description (current)

Updated 2026-06-05 for the v7 redesign + credit system. Plain text — the store field is not HTML. 

WW Extension saves you hours of reading job descriptions by using AI to score WaterlooWorks co-op postings directly against your resume. WaterlooWorks dumps hundreds of postings on you each cycle, but this extension reads each posting, compares it against your candidate profile and preferences using AI, and gives you a clear 1-20 score and verdict so you know exactly which jobs are worth applying to - without opening 200 individual tabs.

Sign in with your Google account to get started. New accounts include free credits to try it out, and you can top up anytime - scoring runs on our backend, so there's no API key to manage.

Features:
* Bulk scan: One click scans every posting in your current search and injects 1-20 scores directly into the job table.
* Ranked sidebar: All your scored jobs, sorted best-first. Click any entry to jump straight to its row, or remove scores you don't need.
* Smart extraction: Upload your CV PDF to the web dashboard and the system extracts your relevant profile text, so you can add custom match criteria.
* Hard filters: Automatically skip postings that violate your strict exclusions (like location or citizenship) to save time and credits.
* Bulk save: Filter by score threshold and verdict, then push matching jobs into a WaterlooWorks folder in one shot.

Note: This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the University of Waterloo or WaterlooWorks.
