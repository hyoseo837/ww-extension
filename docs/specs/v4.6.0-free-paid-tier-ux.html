Spec v4.6 — Free tier + paid tier UX

Goal

v4.5 shipped credit purchasing, so the free tier (100-credit signup bonus) and paid tier (Buy buttons) both exist — but they are miscommunicated and mispriced. v4.6 makes the economics honest and visible, and nudges purchase when a user runs out. Three changes:

  1. Re-price via margin. Raise the per-token margin in pricing.py from 2× to 5× so the free tier is a meaningful trial (~200 scans) rather than a whole term, and a heavy term of scanning costs about $3 of paid usage. The 100-credit ($1 CAD) signup bonus stays unchanged.
  2. Communicate value accurately. Fix the wrong copy and show approximate scan counts on the welcome page and options page, with the credit basis as a light footnote.
  3. Nudge purchase when out. When a scan batch halts on NO_CREDITS, show a "Buy credits" prompt in the sidebar that opens the options Account/Buy section.

Why re-price, and why via margin

Real data from the v4.4 scan table (14 successful Flash scans) puts a typical Flash scan at 0.18 credits (range 0.07–0.23), and a profile extract at ~0.95 credits — both at the original 2× margin. At that rate 100 free credits is ~550 scans, enough to cover an entire co-op term, so almost no user would ever pay. The wrong welcome-page claim ("≈15 Flash scans") was off by ~35×.

Rather than shrink the bonus (a 30–50¢ starter feels worthless), the decision is to raise the margin so the same bonus buys fewer scans. Per-scan credit cost scales linearly with margin (≈ 0.09 × margin credits). At 5×:

Flash scan
~0.45 credits (~½¢) → 100 free credits ≈ ~200 scans.
Profile extract
~2.4 credits.
Packages
$5 / 500cr ≈ ~1,100 scans · $10 / 1000cr ≈ ~2,200 · $20 / 2000cr ≈ ~4,400.
Heavy term
~700–1000 scans ≈ ~$3–4 total, i.e. roughly $3 of paid usage after the free bonus.

The change affects future scans only — existing balances and ledger rows are untouched. Margin lives in code (pricing.py) by design; updating it is a deliberate code-review + redeploy (see spec v4.4.0 Notes). Like the original 2× choice, it is recorded here in Notes and in the pricing.py docstring — not an ADR.

Notes

  • Per-scan cost data source. The 0.18-credit Flash average (range 0.07–0.23) comes from select avg(actual_cost) … from scan where status='success' and model='gemini-2.5-flash' and kind='scan' over 14 live v4.4 scans on 2026-05-29. The copy's scan-count figures are derived from this and rounded to "about" — they vary with posting length, profile size, and model, so the UI never promises exact counts.
  • Verified in production (2026-05-29). After the 5× margin deployed, 12 live Flash scans averaged 0.4485 credits (range 0.36–0.57, total 5.38) — a clean 2.5× over the ~0.18 pre-deploy baseline, exactly as intended. This puts the 100-credit signup bonus at ~220 scans (the "~200, a meaningful trial" target) and confirmed the estimate→debit→refund flow still refunds down to actual: the pre-debit estimates were ~2.2–2.5 credits, refunded to the ~0.45 actual. Source: select avg(actual_cost), sum(actual_cost) from scan where model='gemini-2.5-flash' and kind='scan' and status='success' and created_at > '2026-05-29'.
  • Margin rebalance is not an ADR. The original 2× margin was recorded in spec v4.4.0 Notes and the pricing.py docstring, not an ADR; the v4.6 bump to 5× follows the same convention. The dial is easy to turn again later (one file, code-review + redeploy) as real cost and willingness-to-pay data accrues.
  • Existing balances unaffected. Re-pricing changes only the credits charged on future scans. Users who signed up under the 2× margin keep their balances and effectively got more scans for their bonus — acceptable for a small early user base.
  • Out of scope (deferred). Adjusting the signup bonus amount, model-specific pricing UI, a dedicated pricing page on the external website (whiteboard idea, separate track), and the v5 Account-section consolidation.
