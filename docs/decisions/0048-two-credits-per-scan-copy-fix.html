ADR 0048 — A scan costs ~2 credits now: correct the copy, keep the pricing

Context

ADR 0034 (v7.4) set the per-token MARGIN so a typical scan cost ~1 credit, and "1 credit ≈ 1 scan" became the message across the Buy page, landing pricing, getting-started FAQ, and the extension's scan-confirm estimate. Since then v8.5 rebuilt the match criteria and research-hardened the scoring prompt — more input tokens per scan — and the real charge drifted to roughly double. Owner measurement on production, 2026-06-11: 35 scans cost 67.9 credits (~1.94/scan). The claim and the charge no longer matched, which is exactly the kind of gap that erodes trust in a paid product.

Decision

Keep the pricing as it is (margin, packs, and the 1 credit = $0.01 CAD base rate are unchanged) and correct every surface to say a scan costs about 2 credits: web copy (Buy, landing, getting-started FAQ, pack notes — "≈ 150 scans" for the 300 pack) and the extension's confirm dialog, whose estimate and low-balance warning now use 2 credits/scan. Owner call, 2026-06-11.

Consequences

  • What users are told matches what the ledger charges; the scan-confirm low-balance warning fires when the balance is actually short instead of at half the real cost.
  • ADR 0034's "≈ 1 credit per scan" target is no longer operative (its pack structure and re-margin mechanics stand). Re-margining back down to ~1/scan was considered and rejected: it would halve revenue per scan to preserve a slogan.
  • The ~2 figure is prompt-size-dependent. Any future phase that meaningfully grows or shrinks the scoring prompt must re-measure and update the same copy surfaces (grep for "2 credits").
