ADR 0052 — Pricing refresh: $5/$10/$20 ladder (drop $3 floor, add $20 top tier) and double the signup bonus to 200

Context

ADR 0034 set the bonus-pack economy (token-based per-scan cost via one MARGIN dial; Stripe packs where cad_cents no longer equals credits above the entry tier). v7.4.2 then retuned the tiers to $3/300, $5/525, $10/1100 after live testing showed Stripe's fixed $0.30/charge fee ate a third of the $1 tier.

Two facts have shifted since. First, a real scan now costs ~2 credits, not the ~1 ADR 0034 designed for — the v8.5 richer prompt outgrew it (measured live 2026-06-11: 35 scans → 67.9 credits, see ADR 0048). That quietly halved what every credit balance buys, including the 100-credit signup bonus, which now buys ~50 scans instead of the ~100 the "1 credit ≈ 1 scan" story promised. Second, the September wave (ADR 0042) wants a cleaner, more marketable price ladder and a heavier top tier for power users (a co-op job hunt is hundreds of scans).

The owner confirmed the new numbers on 2026-06-16. As with v7.4.2, this moves amounts, not the model — ADR 0034 stays the conceptual decision and is immutable.

Decision

  • New package ladder $5 / $10 / $20 (the $3 entry tier is dropped; a $20 top tier is added). cad_cents is what Stripe charges; credits is what the ledger grants (ADR 0034's bonus model, unchanged):
    idprice (cad_cents)creditsbonus
    credits_525$5 (500)525+25
    credits_1100$10 (1000)1100+100
    credits_2500$20 (2000)2500+500
    The $5 and $10 tiers keep their existing ids and values verbatim; only the $3 tier leaves and the $20 tier joins. The credits ≥ cad_cents invariant (every pack is worth at least its dollars) still holds.
  • Signup bonus 100 → 200 credits. Doubling it restores the "~100 free scans" welcome at today's ~2 credits/scan rate — the number the bonus was always meant to buy. The amount lives in the grant_signup_bonus() trigger; a new migration (0014) recreates the function with 200, mirroring how 0005 last set it. The one-per-user partial unique index and ADR 0029 re-register suppression are untouched.
  • No code-shape change. The Stripe line item is still inline price_data (no Dashboard products, ADR 0012); the granted amount is still derived server-side from package_id; web (packages.ts) and server (payments.py) tables move together in one commit.

Consequences

  • + Cleaner, rounder $5/$10/$20 ladder; a top tier that covers a full co-op cycle without re-buying.
  • + The free signup bonus is back to ~100 scans of runway — a stronger first impression for the September wave.
  • − The $3 entry point is gone, so the cheapest first purchase doubles to $5. Accepted: the $3 tier's 300 credits (~150 scans) was an awkward middle, and the doubled signup bonus softens the floor for new users.
  • − New accounts cost ~$0.004 more in nominal credit value (200 vs 100 credits), and far less in actual Gemini spend (~$0.001/scan raw). Negligible at the current user base.
  • Existing balances and already-granted 100-credit bonuses are unaffected — the migration only changes future grants. No backfill.
  • Server change → server/pyproject.toml + uv.lock bump (ADR 0010). New migration → applied to Supabase on release. Stripe needs no Dashboard change (inline prices).

Alternatives considered

  • Keep the $3 floor and just add $20 — rejected by the owner in favour of a rounder $5/$10/$20 ladder; the $3/300 tier sat awkwardly between the bonus and the bigger packs.
  • Re-margin instead of doubling the bonus (move MARGIN back toward ~1 credit/scan) — rejected: ADR 0048 deliberately settled the copy on "~2 credits/scan" rather than re-tuning the dial; doubling the bonus restores the welcome runway without re-opening the per-scan price.
