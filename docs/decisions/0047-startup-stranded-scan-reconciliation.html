ADR 0047 — Reconcile stranded pending scans at app startup

Context

A scan debits credits in transaction 1, calls Gemini with no DB lock held, then settles (partial refund + success row) in transaction 2. If the process dies between the two — realistically a DigitalOcean deploy restart during the 1–10 s Gemini call — the scan row stays pending forever and the debit is never refunded (audit 2026-06-11 #2). ADR 0046 schedules the fix for v8.9 but leaves the mechanism open. Candidates: a periodic background task, lazy reconciliation when a replayed scan_id hits the pending row, or a one-shot pass at app startup.

Decision

Reconcile at app startup, in lifespan right after the pool initializes: every scan still pending after a 10-minute cutoff gets its unrefunded scan_debit refunded (amount taken from the actual debit ledger rows, not the scan row) and is marked failed with error='stranded', in one set-based transaction.

Consequences

  • Stranding and recovery are the same event: a scan can only strand when the process dies, and the platform restarts the process — so the refund lands at the moment the cause occurred, with no scheduler, no new dependency, and no per-request cost.
  • The 10-minute cutoff guarantees no in-flight scan (seconds long) can be swept; refunds keyed to actual debit rows and guarded by a no-existing-refund check make a re-run a no-op.
  • Limitation accepted: with a single app instance (current DO setup), a stranded debit stays unrefunded until the next restart or deploy — for this project's deploy cadence that window is short, and the admin grant path remains the manual override. If the app ever runs multiple instances or restarts become rare, revisit with a periodic task (supersede this ADR).
  • The replay path keeps returning 409 for a stranded scan_id until reconciliation runs; the extension never reuses scan ids, so no client behaviour depends on it.
