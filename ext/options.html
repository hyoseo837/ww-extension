WW Extension
How to Use
  1. Sign in with Google under Account below. New accounts receive 100 free credits — about ~200 job scans.
  2. Set up your profile and match preferences on the web app at ww-extension.hyoseo.dev — upload your application package to extract your profile, then set your match criteria.
  3. Navigate to the WaterlooWorks jobs list, click the purple AI Score tab on the right edge, then hit Scan All Jobs.

Extension settings and scoring results are stored locally via chrome.storage.local. Your account, profile, and credits live on your WW Scorer account — manage them at ww-extension.hyoseo.dev. Scoring runs on the backend, which calls Google's Gemini API; job descriptions and your stored profile are sent to the backend per scan.
