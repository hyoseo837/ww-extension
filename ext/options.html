How to use
  1. Sign in with your UWaterloo account under Account below.
  2. Set up your profile on the web app: upload your application package, then set your match preferences.
  3. Open the WaterlooWorks jobs list, click the AI Score tab on the right edge, then Scan All Jobs.

Extension settings and scoring results are stored locally via chrome.storage.local. Your account, profile, and credits live on your WW Scorer account — manage them at ww-extension.hyoseo.dev. Scoring runs on the backend, which calls Google's Gemini API; job descriptions and your stored profile are sent to the backend per scan.
