WW Extension

Welcome!

AI-powered job scoring for WaterlooWorks. Get set up in 3 steps.

1
Sign in with Google
Open Settings (button below), scroll to Account, and click Sign in with Google. New accounts get 100 free credits (about $1 CAD worth — roughly 15 Flash scans) to try the extension. Scoring runs on the backend; you don't need your own Gemini API key.
2
Upload your application package
Go to Profile & Context and drop in your application package PDF (downloadable from the WaterlooWorks Documents page). Click Extract Profile — the backend extracts your info and stores it on your account, then uses it to score each job.
3
Go to WaterlooWorks and scan
Navigate to the co-op jobs list, click the purple AI Score tab on the right edge of the page, then hit Scan All Jobs.
